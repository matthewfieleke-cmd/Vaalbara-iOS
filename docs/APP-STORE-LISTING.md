## Description

```
Vaalbara: The Last Oasis is a strategy battle game with a living orchestral score — and a full Intro to Music Theory classroom built into the app.

THE HOOK
Most games play music in the background. In Vaalbara, the soundtrack is part of the fight: warriors join the orchestra as they enter battle. Eagles sing harmony. Bees hum drones. Fire Ants bite in sixteenth notes. The rock drummer of minutes 4 and 5 drives the climax. You’re not just playing a game — you’re inside a composition in D minor.

INTRO TO MUSIC THEORY
Tap Teach me on the Home Screen and open the Conservatory of Vaalbara. Six short chapters — written at the level of a freshman music major — teach general music theory using the game’s own score as the example set:

• Key Signature — tonic, scales, D minor, the leading tone
• Chord Progression — triads, Roman numerals, i–VI–iv–V, cadences
• Melody — motif, contour, Theme A engraved on a real staff (notes light up as they play)
• Harmony — intervals, voice leading; tap animated Eagle and Bee cards for guided listening
• Time Signature & Rhythm — measures, downbeats, note values, syncopation
• Percussion — the kit, backbeat, fills, half-time, and the suckout-and-slam

Every listening example is synthesized live by the same engine that powers the battle soundtrack. Five check-your-understanding questions close each chapter.

BATTLE & DUELS
Command prehistoric and animal factions across Basalt Fields and the Last Oasis. Deploy warriors, manage lanes, and feel the score intensify with the fight. Duels offer focused one-on-one matchups. Offline vs bot — play anywhere, no account required.

HEADPHONES RECOMMENDED
The score is the soul of the game. What you hear is what you learn.

Vaalbara Games — Mobile Game of the Year ambitions, music classroom included.
```
