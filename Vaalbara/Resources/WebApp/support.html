Vaalbara Support

Need help with Vaalbara: The Last Oasis? Contact Vaalbara Games at matthew.fieleke@gmail.com.

When contacting support

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and a brief description of what happened. Screenshots are helpful when available. Never send passwords, payment information, or other sensitive data.

Local game data

Match history and preferences are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes that local data. Vaalbara Games cannot recover deleted profiles or results.
