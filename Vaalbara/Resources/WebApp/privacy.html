Vaalbara Privacy Policy

Effective July 13, 2026

Vaalbara Games respects your privacy. This policy explains how Vaalbara: The Last Oasis handles information in its first App Store release.

Information stored on your device

Vaalbara stores a generated guest name, match results, preferred faction, and bot-strength setting locally on your device. This information supports your profile and preferences. Vaalbara Games does not receive it, and it is removed when you delete the app or clear its local data.

Information we collect

Vaalbara Games does not collect personal information or gameplay data from this release. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, or online multiplayer services. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or advertising identifier.

Third parties

This release does not send information to third-party analytics, advertising, or gameplay services. Standard App Store distribution is governed by Apple’s own privacy practices.

Children’s privacy

Because Vaalbara Games does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Future changes

If a future release introduces online features or data collection, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before those features are made available.

Contact

For privacy questions, email matthew.fieleke@gmail.com. You can also visit the Vaalbara support page.
